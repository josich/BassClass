## Wrong        (incorrect musical or factual information)

### W1. Staff view: many rhythms are drawn with the wrong values, rests all look the same, and there is no time signature
- Where: the "Tab / staff" toggle under ⋯. It works on every exercise and is saved as a setting. The Reading module itself is correct (only 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2 and 4 beats).
- What I saw:
  - **Dotted 8th** (0.75) is drawn as a filled head + dot + no flag, i.e. a **dotted quarter**.
    - #29 "Dotted 8th + 16th" in the app (DOM): `filled dot | filled flags2`, ×4 per bar. The bar reads as 4 × (1.5 + 0.25) = 7 beats in 4/4.
    - Same in #64 16th grid (16 notes), #97 displacement, #126 neo-soul.
  - **Triplets** (1/3) are drawn as plain 8ths and **quarter-note triplets** (2/3) as quarters, with no "3".
    - #4, #63, and the 18 triad exercises whose default subdivision is triplets.
    - Swung 8ths in all licks (2/3 + 1/3), #28 and #163 are drawn as **quarter + 8th** pairs, so a swung bar of 8ths looks like 6 beats. A jazz chart writes straight 8ths with "Swing".
  - Durations 1.25, 1.75, 2.25, 2.5, 3.5 and 3.75 (#30 syncopation, #97) are drawn as quarters or halves. They need ties.
  - **Every rest** is the same small block on the middle line (a half-rest shape), whatever its length (code: `srest` 8×3 px). #5 Notes and rests shows quarter rests as half rests.
  - **No time signature** is drawn at all, also in 5/4, 7/8, 6/8 and 3/4 (#98, #127, #134 4:3, #144 in 3/4).
- Measured: with default options, 35 exercises contain durations that the staff draws as a different value.
- Why it matters: a staff that shows the wrong rhythm is worse than no staff for a reader. The decision "simple stems and flags, no beams" does not cover wrong values.
- Suggestion: draw a flag on dotted 8ths; add a "3" over tuplets; tie or split odd durations; use a rest glyph per length; draw the time signature. Or offer the staff only where the rhythms are representable.
