## Major

### M1. The Piano plays every note one octave too high
- Where: `web/audio/piano/*.ogg` and `piano.json`, made by `web/js/dev/pianoprep.js:14-17` (`NOTES` 'D3'…'A5' named with `midiOf`, C4 = 60).
- Evidence 1, the files themselves: in each file the labelled fundamental is 54-74 dB below the strongest partial, which sits at twice that frequency, and the labelled 3rd partial is missing too (FFT from 0.1 s, 32k points):

  | File | Labelled pitch | f | 2f | 3f | 4f |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 50.ogg | D3 146.8 Hz | −53.7 | 0 | −55.9 | 0 |
  | 60.ogg | C4 261.6 Hz | −57.8 | 0 | −58.0 | −18.4 |
  | 64.ogg | E4 329.6 Hz | −57.6 | 0 | −63.5 | −21.9 |
  | 72.ogg | C5 523.3 Hz | −61.4 | 0 | −65.8 | −33.8 |
  | 81.ogg | A5 880 Hz | −60.4 | 0 | −74.6 | −44.3 |

  Only even partials of the labelled note are present: the recordings sound one octave above their labels. `yin` (fmin 100, fmax 1200) reads +1195 to +1210 cents on 50-75.ogg.
- Evidence 2, through the app: `padSet([57], t, 'piano')` (A3, 220 Hz), rendered offline through the real graph with the piano loaded: 220 Hz −62.1 dB, 440 Hz 0 dB, 660 Hz −63 dB. The same call with pad, e-piano or organ: 220 Hz is the strongest.
- Result: every chord and drone the room plays with Piano, and every chord chip tapped with Piano, sounds an octave above the other sounds. That breaks the room's register rule (chord roots E3-D♯4, above the bass).
- Fix idea:
  - label each file 12 higher, or better, prepare the samples an octave lower (VCSL's D2-A4), so E3-D♯4 roots are near recorded notes;
  - then measure the loudness again. Today the piano is −3.7 dB A-weighted under the pad (pad −17.45, piano −21.18, e-piano −18.45, organ −17.59, open guitar −16.77; harmony 100%, three chords of 2.5 s).

### M2. Pro exercises open without Pro through two address forms (no in-app link leads there)
- Where: `web/js/pro.js:139-149` `hashLock`, `pro.js:88-93` `exerciseLock(t, ls)`, `web/js/app.js:94` route.
- Two holes:
  1. **An exercise number.** The router opens `#/ex/<number>` (`byNumber`), but `hashLock` looks up `byId` only, so it returns null. Result: 0 of the 115 Pro exercises are locked by number.
  2. **Any free lesson's `ls`.** With `ls=<free lesson>.<n>`, `exerciseLock` returns `lessonLock(L)` (null for a free lesson) and never checks that step n is this exercise. The router then sees the mismatch, drops the lesson and opens the plain exercise with all its options. All 115 Pro exercises open with `?ls=hold.0` or `?ls=hold.999`.
- Steps (gates On, not owned):
  - `location.hash = '#/ex/116'` opens "Key centres: every note of a key in one position" (Level 4): `body.in-ex` true, no Pro sheet.
  - `location.hash = '#/ex/fb-key-box?ls=hold.0'` opens the same exercise.
  - `#/ex/fb-key-box` (by id) correctly opens the Pro sheet: "🔒 Level 4 is part of BassClass Pro".
- Reach: typed or pasted addresses only. No screen generates such a link: Library search "#116" shows a locked card, and the card goes by id. The APK has no address bar and no deep-link intent filter.
- No free lesson uses a Pro exercise (0 of 119 free lessons' steps), so the "steps of a free lesson always play" exception is never needed today.
- Fix idea:
  - in `hashLock`, resolve numbers as the router does;
  - in `exerciseLock`, honour `ls` only when `L.steps[n]?.ex === t.id`.

### M4. Fills play straight 16ths over a swung groove (Triplets on a straight pattern)
- Where: `web/js/practice/styles.js:449` `phraseHits`: `const g = s.grid === 3 || s.beat === 'dq' ? 3 : s.beat === 'e' ? 2 : bpm <= 150 ? 4 : 2;`. The fill's grid ignores the subdivision `L`. With Triplets, a straight 16th pattern is swung onto the triplet grid (`stepTime`), and so are the clicks.
- Evidence (`__fills19`, 2,700 cases):
  - In 88 straight (grid 4) patterns at L = 3, the fill bar's beats put the groove at 0, 0.333, 0.667 and the fill at 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75. Example: rock8, 80 BPM, every 4 bars.
  - All 88: rock8 … sevenfour, every straight pattern that offers Triplets.
- Fine:
  - Triplet-grid and compound patterns fill in triplets / 8ths of the dotted beat.
  - No hit outside its beat, the kick never lost under a fill, a crash on every phrase start (jig has no snare or kick, so no crash: by design).
- Fix idea: on L = 3, take the fill's places from the triplet grid (`stepTime`/`clickTimes`), as the arpeggio and the chugs already do.

### M5. The organ's Leslie and the guitar's amp run all the time: the idle audio graph costs about 5x what it did
- Where: `web/js/audio.js` `audioCtx()`. `leslieIn = leslie(ac, padRaw)` starts 4 LFO oscillators, and `ampIn = amp(ac, padRaw)` builds two 4x-oversampled WaveShapers, 9 biquads and a 0.35 s stereo convolver.
  - They are built and running from the first sound of the session. A tap tick is enough: `tapTick()` calls `audioCtx()`.
  - The AudioContext is never suspended.
- Evidence: offline render time of 20 s of silence on this PC, 3 runs each.
  - Whole graph at 3219994: 979 / 998 / 1001 ms, about 5% of real time (one earlier run, with other work in the tab: 453 ms).
  - f15a4eb's graph (frozen copy on :8883): 162 / 219 / 223 ms, about 1%.
  - The two pieces alone, rebuilt in a bare context: amp 450-467 ms (one run 172), Leslie 128-134 ms, both 587-642 ms. A bare context: 4-5 ms.
- On a phone several times slower this is a steady load (and battery) whenever the app is open, even for players who never pick Organ or Guitar. Suspicion for phones, not measured on a device.
- Fix idea: build the Leslie and the amp the first time Organ or Guitar is picked, and disconnect or stop them when another sound is chosen or the room closes. Suspending the context when nothing has played for a while would also help.

### M6. A backup carries the Pro record: an edited (or someone else's) backup turns Pro on
- Where: `web/js/backup.js` saves and restores every `bassclass.*` key, including `bassclass.pro.v1`. `pro.js` `owned()` trusts `{ owned: true, src: 'play' }` on the Play build (`'test'` elsewhere).
- Steps (test build, gates On, not owned):
  1. `backupText()`, set `bassclass.pro.v1` to `{"owned":true,"src":"test","miss":null}`.
  2. `parseBackup` → `restoreBackup`.
  3. `isPro()` → true.
- On Play the same works with `src: "play"`, a one-word edit of the JSON file.
- `syncPro` then takes it back only after two checks a day apart. Never while Google Play cannot be reached: an error changes nothing. Restoring again resets it.
- The plan accepts "a shared or pirated copy … rather than fought". This is reachable from the UI with a text editor, so the user may want to decide.
- Fix idea:
  - leave `bassclass.pro.v1` out of restores and keep the device's own;
  - or restore it with `miss` set to now, so Play must confirm it.

### m5. About: legal texts against the app's real behaviour
- "Your progress stays on your device … stored only on your phone … Uninstalling the app … deletes them".
  - `AndroidManifest.xml` has `android:allowBackup="true"`. Android's Auto Backup copies the app's data (the WebView's storage included) to the user's Google account backup, and restores it on a reinstall.
  - Either say so, or set `allowBackup="false"` / backup rules.
- The "Terms of the purchase" and "Pro opens Levels 4–6 …" also show in the web version and the GitHub APK, where nothing is locked or sold.
- "Tips … are not refundable once used, as Google Play allows" is a legal claim to check before launch (not verified here).
