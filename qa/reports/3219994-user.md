## Wrong        (incorrect musical or factual information)

### W1. The Guitar plays a power chord with a perfect 5th over chords whose 5th is flat or sharp
- **Where:** Practice room → Harmony → Chords → Sound "Guitar" (Pro).
- **Why:** `PAD_SOUNDS.guitar.chord` is `[root−12, root−5, root]`, whatever the chord's quality.
- **Evidence:**
  - Minor ii – V – i in C: the Dm7♭5 chord is `D4 F4 A♭4 C5`, but the guitar plays `D3 A3 D4`. A♮ against A♭.
  - Jazz blues in C: the guitar plays F♯°7 as F♯ C♯ F♯; the chord's 5th is C.
  - The relative-minor cycle: the guitar plays Bm7♭5 with F♯.
  - My progression offers ° + ø7 °7 7alt: every one of them gets a perfect 5th.
- **Why it matters:**
  - The Neck shows A♭ (Chord view) while the guitar sounds A♮ a half step away.
  - Over a ° or ø chord this is not a matter of style: it is the wrong chord.
- **Suggestion:** take the chord's own 5th (♭5 or ♯5), or play root and octave when the chord has no perfect 5th.

### W2. The Pro list says every Pro exercise is "in every key"; 24 of the 115 have no key at all
- **Where:** Settings → BassClass Pro ⓘ. The text reads: "📚 Library Levels 4–6: 115 more exercises, each in every key with its own variations".
- **Evidence:** these Pro exercises have no key, root or tonic option:
  - the rhythm drills: `rh-groupings`, `rh-displacement`, `rh-odd-meters`, `rh-sparse-click`, `rh-polyrhythm`;
  - the technique drills: `tech-stretch`, `tech-harmonics`, `tech-permutations-across`;
  - `ear-progressions`;
  - all 15 song studies, which keep their song's key.
- **Why it matters:** it is a sales promise the content does not keep.
- **Suggestion:** "115 more exercises, most in every key, each with its own variations".

### W3. About says "BassClass uses no third-party code", but the APK ships AndroidX WebKit
- **Where:** About → 🎼 The content.
- **Evidence:**
  - `android/app/build.gradle.kts` depends on `androidx.webkit:webkit:1.12.1`, under the Apache License 2.0.
  - The Kotlin standard library comes with the Kotlin build too.
  - The Play Billing Library will be added for the store build (`billing.js`, LAUNCH-TODO).
  - The Apache 2.0 licence asks for its notice to ship with the app; "Licence files" lists only the three sound licences.
- **Suggestion:**
  - Say "no tracking, ads or third-party web code".
  - Add an "Open-source libraries" fold with the AndroidX and Kotlin notices (and Billing's, when it lands).
