## Major

### M1. Backing pad: a loud distorted burst when playback stops or changes just before a chord change
- Where: `web/js/audio.js:352-385` (`padStop`, `padSet`). Callers:
  - `player.js:129` (`pause`, which also runs on leaving the exercise and on every option change, via `load` → `stop`);
  - `exercise.js:377` (`applyBacking` when the Backing button is cycled during playback).
- Cause:
  - The scheduler queues the next chord up to 0.12 s ahead. `padSet` gives each new voice a fresh `GainNode`, whose own value is 1. It schedules `setValueAtTime(0, when)` and `setTargetAtTime(level, when, 0.12)`, with `level = 0.16/√n`.
  - If `padStop(t)` runs with `t < when`, `cancelScheduledValues(t)` deletes both events. The gain falls back to 1.0. The oscillators still start at `when` (they stop at `t + 1.2`).
  - So the release `setTargetAtTime(0, t, 0.15)` starts from 1.0, which is 6–12 times the pad's level.
- Steps:
  1. Turn Backing to Chords (under ⋯).
  2. Open `#/ex/arp-rhythm-changes`, set 200 BPM and press Play.
  3. Change the key in Options a few times while it plays (or press pause, or leave the exercise).
- Expected / Actual:
  - Expected: the pad fades out or crossfades.
  - Actual: about 1 stop in 12 makes a loud burst of roughly 0.3 s. It goes over the output soft clipper's ceiling, so it is distorted.
- Evidence:
  - Offline replica of the same calls:
    - normal pad peak 0.19;
    - `padStop` 80 ms before a scheduled chord: 1.47;
    - before the first chord: 1.78.
  - Live app (tap on the clipper input; a normal pad peaks at ≤0.2, or 0.53 during fast crossfades):

    | Action | Pad peak | Clipper input |
    |---|---|---|
    | Pause right after a chord was queued | 1.004 | 1.48 |
    | Leaving the exercise 20 ms before a chord (not forced) | 1.83 | – |
    | Final pause of a 3-min run at 260 BPM | 1.445 | 2.14 |
    | 24 key changes at 200 BPM | 2 bursts: 2.18 and 1.47 | 2.64 and 1.98 |
  - Chance per stop ≈ 0.12 s divided by the chord length:
    - about 4% with one chord per bar at 84 BPM;
    - 20–26% with two chords per bar at 200–260 BPM.
- Fix idea: create the voice gain at 0 (`g.gain.value = 0`) before scheduling, or use `cancelAndHoldAtTime`.

### M9. `gr-genres` (#88) country two-beat contradicts its own text
- About: "alternates root and 5th on beats 1 and 3, with a walk-up into the next chord". Tip: "keep beats 2 and 4 empty for the snare".
- Actual: quarter notes on all four beats (R 5 R 5).
- The walk-up bar (R 5 2 3) never sounds with the style's progression (I–IV–V–I, one bar per chord). `grooveLine` restarts the pattern at bar 1 for each chord (`common.js:385`, `let barIdx = 0` inside the chord loop), so bar 2 of a pattern only plays on chords longer than a bar.
