## Blocking     (a user cannot do what the screen is for)

### B1. The tuner cannot read the lowest string of the tunings it offers below about 26 Hz (including F♯ standard on a 6-string)
- **Where:** Tuner, 5- and 6-string basses, Standard ‹ down to G0/F♯0/F0, and Drop down to G0/F♯0/F0/E0/E♭0.
- **What I did:**
  - Set a 6-string to F♯ standard (‹ five times: F♯0 B0 E1 A1 D2 G2, the owner's own example), started the tuner and tapped the F♯0 string.
  - Fed it a sawtooth at exactly 23.12 Hz.
  - Fed it the same sawtooth through two 45 Hz high-passes, as a phone microphone loses the fundamental.
- **What I saw:**
  - No reading at all: the note stays grey ("idle").
  - The same chain read 30.87 Hz (B0) and 25.96 Hz (A♭0) correctly, and 46.25 Hz was folded onto F♯0 "+0 cents".
  - 24.5 Hz and 23.12 Hz: nothing.
  - In Auto mode, 23.12 Hz with the fundamental intact also gave nothing.
- **Why:**
  - `yin()` in `pitch.js` stops at `fmin = 26` Hz (τ ≤ 461 samples at 12 kHz), so it never sees a 23 Hz period.
  - The waveform's real period stays 43 ms even when the fundamental is weak, because the 3rd harmonic is not a multiple of the 2nd.
  - There is also the tuner's 22 Hz high-pass.
- **Tunings affected:**
  - 5- and 6-string: G, F♯ and F standard; Drop G, F♯, F, E and E♭.
  - On a 4-string, Drop A♭ (25.96 Hz) is at the edge.
- **Evidence:** the 6-string string row "F♯0 23.1 Hz | B0 30.9 Hz | …" (…\mcp-Claude_Browser-blob-1793289268620-hrpmxg.jpg); readings logged per frequency in my session.
- **Suggestion:** lower `fmin` (about 18 Hz, with a longer analysis window) for the chosen string, or stop the ‹ range where the detector ends.

### W7. #349 tip: the counting of the gallop is wrong
- **Where:** #349 tips (drawer).
- **Evidence:** 'Count "1 – 2-and" in your head: the 16ths sit on "2-and".'
